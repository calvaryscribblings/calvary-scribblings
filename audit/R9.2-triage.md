## §4 — THE RANKED QUEUE

### FIX BEFORE LAUNCH — by severity × blast radius

**1 · PL-12 — the build depends on a live, unauthenticated Firebase read.**
*Blast radius: every deploy, including the launch-day one.* A Firebase blip during a Cloudflare
Pages build either fails the build (the wall generator throws) or ships a `_redirects` missing
154 story routes (the redirect generator swallows it). One of those two behaviours is wrong and
they disagree with each other.
**Scope:** a shared `fetchWithRetry(url, {attempts: 3, backoff})` used by both generators, and
one deliberate answer to "should a missing catalogue fail the build or degrade it" — applied
consistently. `generate-gateway-wall.mjs:84`'s `fetch(story.cover)` per cover wants a timeout in
the same pass. **Size: half a round.** Ranked first because it is the only item on this list that
can break the launch deploy itself.

**2 · PL-9 residual — `public/sw.js` has no test of any kind.**
*Blast radius: one bad edit strands every returning reader.* The file's own header calls it *"the
riskiest thing in the repo"*; it is in both workflows' trigger paths and neither runs anything
that looks at it.
**Scope:** a suite over the pure predicates (`isPassThrough`, `isShelfPath`, `shouldCache`,
the non-GET short-circuit) plus a harness that drives `fetch` events against a stub cache. The
route-map fence and the `/bookstore` exclusion are the two assertions that matter. **Size: one
round.**

**3 · PL-9 residual — `functions/api/` outside `bookstore/` is untested.**
*Blast radius: eight endpoints, three of them token-verifying.* Lower than the two above because
none is on the money path, and `timeouts.test.mjs` has proved the `onRequestPost` harness pattern
works. **Scope:** the same stub-fetch harness applied to `hit.js`, `newsletter/*` and `admin/*`
first. **Size: one round, or fold into whichever workstream next touches one.**
