## The leading, as computed

The gap between a descriptor's lines equals the gap between its words. Measured by the engine at 54px, tracking 9, by `descriptorLeading()` in render.mjs:

- cap height (capAscent of "H"): **35.000px**
- word gap as drawn (the two spaces of the separator and the three tracking steps around them, measured as "H  H" less two "H"s): **48.600px**
- **leading = 83.600px, 1.5481 of the size.** Baseline to baseline; so one line's baseline to the next line's cap tops is the word gap.

`DESCRIPTOR.lineHeight` (1.12) is removed from layout.mjs: the leading is this derivation, and no second number is left.

## The space the rule takes

A second descriptor line adds 83.6px between the descriptor and the fleuron. The fleuron keeps its rule: its ink is centred at 42% of the gap between the descriptor's LAST line and the author's cap top. The author and footer are pinned to the canvas and do not move.

**No element is pushed past the space it has.** Every stack still fits its region, no descriptor moves a title off its rung, and every descriptor line is inside the 1232px measure.

## Title changes (the hyphen rule)

A break at an existing hyphen is allowed only where it leaves at least 2 letters or numerals of the word behind and carries at least 3 forward, counted across the whole word on each side (`HYPHEN_MIN` in text.mjs). A refused break is handled like any break that doesn't fit: the ladder steps down.

| Title | Old rung | Old lines | New rung | New lines | Descriptor too? |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| Can Marvel's New X-Men Escape the Shadow of Hugh Jackman and the Original Cast? | 78px | CAN MARVEL'S NEW X- / MEN ESCAPE THE SHADOW / OF HUGH JACKMAN AND / THE ORIGINAL CAST? | 68px | CAN MARVEL'S NEW X-MEN / ESCAPE THE SHADOW OF / HUGH JACKMAN AND THE / ORIGINAL CAST? | no |
| Public Wi-Fi Is a Trap | 186px | PUBLIC WI- / FI IS A TRAP | 140px | PUBLIC WI-FI IS / A TRAP | yes |

**Hyphen breaks that are kept** (4): *Become a Content Creator: The Step-by-Step Guide* (BECOME A CONTENT / CREATOR: THE STEP- / BY-STEP GUIDE); *Brown-Skinned Girl* (BROWN- / SKINNED GIRL); *Red-White-Red* (RED-WHITE- / RED); *The Ad Tug-of-War* (THE AD TUG- / OF-WAR).

## Borderline

The measure is applied exactly, with no tolerance. 2 covers are over it by less than 2px and so break: *SPOTLIGHT: Chinedu Philip Onoyima — ‘I want you to know that I’m coming’* (1.13px over); *One Day for the Thief* (0.04px over). A tolerance would be a new, chosen number, so none is added.

## Chosen rather than derived

- **The hyphen minimums, 2 behind and 3 forward** (Bringhurst's rule): Ikenna's ruling, chosen, not derived.
- **Letters or numerals are what count toward the minimums**; the hyphens themselves and any punctuation do not.
- **A single descriptor word wider than the measure raises an error** rather than being drawn, as an impossible title does.
- **The two probes proposed above**, and the sheet's layout.

Derived: the measure (the title's, 1232px), the break (the title's own fill-the-line wrap), the leading (cap height plus word gap, from the engine), the size and tracking (unchanged), and the fleuron's 42%. R19's closest-widths chooser and its tie-breaks are gone.
