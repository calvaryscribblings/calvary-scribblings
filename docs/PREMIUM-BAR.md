# The Premium Bar

Every finding cites the line it fails.

1. STATES: every screen has a designed loading, empty, error, offline and slow state. No blank boards, no raw error text, no dead ends, no spinner where cached content could show.
2. ALIGNMENT: one grid per surface; edges and baselines line up; rows never go ragged; spacing on the 8pt scale; numbers derived, not chosen.
3. TYPE: house faces only (Cormorant Garamond, Cinzel); oldstyle figures in counts; a comfortable measure; no widows in headings; web prose uses text-wrap: pretty and headings text-wrap: balance where supported; Dynamic Type and browser zoom never break a layout.
4. MOTION: one grammar (the app's spring family and duration ladder, and the web's equivalents); motion serves reading, never decoration; Reduce Motion honoured.
5. TOUCH: 44pt minimum targets; every tap acknowledged within 100ms; optimistic updates show in-flight and failed states with a retry; nothing fails silently.
6. SPEED: the app scrolls at the display's full rate on an iPhone and a mid-range Android; cold start and tab switches measured. Web: LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP ≤ 200ms, CLS ≤ 0.1 at p75, from field data (CrUX/PageSpeed) where it exists, otherwise lab, labelled which.
7. ACCESS: VoiceOver/TalkBack labels and order, AA contrast, visible focus on web, Reduce Transparency honoured.
8. PLATFORM: iOS glass on controls only, correct at both ends of iOS 27's glass slider; Android 16 edge-to-edge insets and predictive back correct (Play has required targetSdk 36 since 31 Aug).
9. PARITY: web and app show the same content and behaviour unless a ruled divergence is recorded.
10. MONEY WALL: no price, buy or purchase language anywhere on iOS.
11. COPY: British English and house voice; every message says what happened and what to do next.
