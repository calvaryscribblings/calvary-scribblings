## Corner indices

- **Region, chosen:** a fanned hand held in the left hand shows each card's left edge. The index sits in the top-left 12 × 16 mm of the trim, and its 180° twin in the bottom-right. Orange on panel 2.
- **Glyph box:** 5.46–11.46 mm across, 5.46–11.46 mm down. That is 0.90 mm inside the inner keyline. C
- **Livery mark:** 8.5 pt Cormorant 600, its cap top 0.9 mm under the glyph. Marks: Short Story **S** · Flash Fiction **F** · Poetry **P** · Inspiring **I** · Series **the house fleuron**. C
- **The livery is carried by a letter, not by colour.** The three dark grounds (#080610, #3C101A, #102E26) will not separate in a fan, or for a colour-blind player. Series takes the fleuron because it is the picture card. An Ending carries its two glyphs side by side, and the Ring carries the eight-point ring mark.
- **Collisions asserted on every rendered card:** no two marks within 0.5 mm of each other, and none outside the inner keyline less 0.5 mm. All 46 deck cards, both Endings shown, the Ring and its names pass. The render throws if any fail.

## The card code

- **Placement, chosen:** the right side, with the panel's right edge at 51.44 mm and its foot 1.2 mm above the bottom-right index and never closer than 0.8 mm to the author line's ascenders (a long author line reaches under the code). The same position on every card. The top right is too close to a wide eyebrow (FLASH FICTION runs to 41.6 mm). The bottom left is too close to a long author line.
- **Module:** 6 device px = **0.254 mm**, whole pixels at 600 dpi. Quiet zone 4 modules (the spec). Error correction M.
- **Decoder:** jsQR 1.4.0 with `inversionAttempts: 'dontInvert'`, run on the full 600-dpi render.
- **Gold on a dark ground fails.** The modules are lighter than the ground, which is an inverted code, and the decoder does not try inversion. So **the dark liveries (Short Story, Flash Fiction, Inspiring) carry the code on a small cream panel** (#F5F0E8), with modules in the livery's own ground colour. **Poetry and Series decode directly**, with modules in their keyline colour on their light ground and no panel.

## Endings, the Ring, and the backs

- **Ending ground, chosen:** house umber **#2A2318** (the site's #2a2318), with furniture in display gold #C9A84C. It is not a purple, and it is distinct from every story livery and from the story back's icon purple.
- **Ending face:** eyebrow ENDING, the name as title on the cover's ladder, then the two glyphs with their names (6 pt) where the descriptor sits, the fleuron, and footer THE TURN. There is no author row. Its index is the two glyphs side by side.
- **Ring:** glyphs on a 12.5 mm radius, clockwise from the top: Letter, Secret, Child, Storm, Road, House, Debt, Fire. Each Ending is named on a 19.6 mm radius, between its two neighbours: two lines, running along the ring, and turned on the lower half to read from the foot. Eyebrow THE RING, footer THE TURN (C).
- **Ending back (shared by the Ring):** eight points (1.1 mm radius) on a 13 mm circle with a hairline, in display gold, centred on the Ending ground. Nothing comes within 14.40 mm of the trim. No glow, and the grain is written in half-turn pairs. **Half-turn symmetry is checked pixel by pixel on the 600-dpi raster: identical.**
- **Story back: NOT BUILT. Stopped at this item.** The brief sources it from the app repo's icon master. This Codespace's GitHub token sees only `calvaryscribblings/calvary-scribblings`, and the app repository is not on disk (see CLAUDE.md). So the icon's pixel size and its effective dpi cannot be reported, and its purple cannot be sampled. A placeholder sits in its slot on the sheet.

## Ground treatment at card size

The glow and vignette keep their proportions to the trim and run on into the bleed. Their blur scales by width. The grain keeps the cover's coverage: 130,433 specks over 3,339,072 px (3.9%), drawn from the cover's slug-seeded stream (D).
