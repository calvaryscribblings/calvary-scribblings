### Descriptors that break: as on the covers

**The face draws the descriptor the cover plans** (COVERS-R19/R20, on main since 2b2a551f, merged into this branch): `planCover`'s descriptor lines, imported, with `descriptorLeading()` between them, also imported. A descriptor wider than the title's measure (1232 cover px, 43.89 mm) breaks the way a title does, filling the first line. Size and tracking never change. The leading is cap height 35.0 + word gap 48.6 = **83.6 cover px (2.978 mm)**. The LAST line takes the single line's place in the stack: it is the stack's foot, the baseline the fleuron's gap is measured from, with the earlier lines one leading apart above it. The stack makes room as the cover's does: one extra line adds one leading to the stack, which still sits at 20% of its slack, so its top rises by 20% of the leading (0.596 mm) and its foot falls by 80% (2.383 mm). The fleuron keeps its 42% of the gap below the last line.

**R1.2's exception is gone.** A descriptor must now clear the inner keyline by 0.5 mm like every other mark, or the render fails. Every one of the 50 story cards passes.

## Corner indices

- **Region, chosen:** a fanned hand held in the left hand shows each card's left edge. The index sits in the top-left 12 × 16 mm of the trim, and its 180° twin in the bottom-right. Orange on panel 2.
- **Glyph box:** 5.46–11.46 mm across, 5.46–11.46 mm down. That is 0.90 mm inside the inner keyline. The bottom-right twin's glyph box is 45.54–51.54 mm across, 77.54–83.54 mm down. C
- **Livery mark (story cards):** 8.5 pt Cormorant 600, its cap top 0.9 mm under the glyph. Marks: Short Story **S** · Flash Fiction **F** · Poetry **P** · Inspiring **I**. C
- **The livery is carried by a letter, not by colour.** The three dark grounds (#080610, #3C101A, #102E26) will not separate in a fan, or for a colour-blind player. A picture card carries the glyph alone (below): the poster says Series. An Ending carries its two glyphs side by side, and the Ring carries the eight-point ring mark.
- **Collisions asserted on every rendered card:** no two marks within 0.5 mm of each other, and none outside the inner keyline less 0.5 mm (the safe line, on a picture card). All 53 deck cards, the 8 Endings, the Ring and its names pass. The render throws if any fail.

## The card code

- **Story cards, placement:** the right side. **R1.3: the panel's right edge is on the bottom-right index glyph box's right edge, 51.54 mm** (0.90 mm inside the inner keyline; it was 51.44 mm). Nothing else moved: the foot is 1.2 mm above the bottom-right index and never closer than 0.8 mm to the author line's ascenders, at **72.45 mm**. So a panel spans 51.54 mm less its side across, and 72.45 mm less its side down.
  - Story code sizes in the deck: v4 10.41 mm, v5 11.43 mm. For example a 10.41 mm panel spans 41.13–51.54 mm × 62.04–72.45 mm.

## Ground treatment at card size

The glow and vignette keep their proportions to the trim and run on into the bleed. Their blur scales by width. The grain keeps the cover's coverage: 130,433 specks over 3,339,072 px (3.9%), drawn from the cover's slug-seeded stream (D).
