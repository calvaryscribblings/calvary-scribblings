### The descriptor: one tracking for the deck

At 6 pt the descriptor is 59.42 cover px. Holding the cover's own tracking as a proportion of size would give 9.90 px, and 31 of the 46 deck descriptors would then outrun the 43.89 mm measure. R1 tightened each of those on its own. **R1.1 sets one value for every card, derived from the longest descriptor in the deck:**

- **The longest descriptor:** "ORPHANHOOD.  SHRINKING.  GRATITUDE.". It is the widest at 6 pt untracked, so it is the one that binds.
- **Deck tracking: 0.53 px between clusters at the 59.42-px (6 pt) size.** That is **0.0089 em**, or 0.019 mm. At this tracking the longest descriptor sets exactly to the measure (43.89 mm), and every other descriptor is narrower.
- **In the cover's tracking units:** the cover tracks its 54-px descriptor at 9 px (0.1667 em). The deck value is 0.48 px at the cover's 54 px, which is 5% of the cover's tracking.

## Corner indices

- **Region, chosen:** a fanned hand held in the left hand shows each card's left edge. The index sits in the top-left 12 × 16 mm of the trim, and its 180° twin in the bottom-right. Orange on panel 2.
- **Glyph box:** 5.46–11.46 mm across, 5.46–11.46 mm down. That is 0.90 mm inside the inner keyline. C
- **Livery mark:** 8.5 pt Cormorant 600, its cap top 0.9 mm under the glyph. Marks: Short Story **S** · Flash Fiction **F** · Poetry **P** · Inspiring **I** · Series **the house fleuron**. C
- **The livery is carried by a letter, not by colour.** The three dark grounds (#080610, #3C101A, #102E26) will not separate in a fan, or for a colour-blind player. Series takes the fleuron because it is the picture card. An Ending carries its two glyphs side by side, and the Ring carries the eight-point ring mark.
- **Collisions asserted on every rendered card:** no two marks within 0.5 mm of each other, and none outside the inner keyline less 0.5 mm. All 50 deck cards, both Endings shown, the Ring and its names pass. The render throws if any fail.

## The card code

- **Placement, chosen:** the right side, with the panel's right edge at 51.44 mm and its foot 1.2 mm above the bottom-right index and never closer than 0.8 mm to the author line's ascenders (a long author line reaches under the code). The same position on every card. The top right is too close to a wide eyebrow (FLASH FICTION runs to 41.6 mm). The bottom left is too close to a long author line.
- **Module:** 6 device px = **0.254 mm**, whole pixels at 600 dpi. Quiet zone 4 modules (the spec). Error correction M.
- **Decoder:** jsQR 1.4.0 with `inversionAttempts: 'dontInvert'`, run on the full 600-dpi render.
- **Gold on a dark ground fails.** The modules are lighter than the ground, which is an inverted code, and the decoder does not try inversion. So **the dark liveries (Short Story, Flash Fiction, Inspiring) carry the code on a small cream panel** (#F5F0E8), with modules in the livery's own ground colour. **Poetry decodes directly**, with modules in its keyline colour on its light ground and no panel. **Picture cards carry the code on a plate** (below), with modules in the plate ink.

## Endings, the Ring, and the backs

No glow, and the grain is written in half-turn pairs. **Half-turn symmetry is checked pixel by pixel on the 600-dpi raster: identical.**
- **Story back:** Ikenna's `cards/Story-Island-icon.png`, taken from where he put it in the Codespace. It was on no branch and not on origin/main, so it is committed on this branch under his name for it.
  - **Source:** 1206 × 1177 px, RGB with no alpha channel (minimum alpha 255). It has no transparency, no rounded corners, and is not square.
  - **Ground:** every one of its 4,762 edge pixels is **#2D0A5E** (rgb 45, 10, 94), a worst deviation of 0. The whole ground outside the mark was also sampled: about 263,000 pixels, every one the same colour. So the ground is flat, and the purple runs to the bleed with no tile edge. ## Ground treatment at card size

The glow and vignette keep their proportions to the trim and run on into the bleed. Their blur scales by width. The grain keeps the cover's coverage: 130,433 specks over 3,339,072 px (3.9%), drawn from the cover's slug-seeded stream (D).
