# Shorelines: the playtest paper (CARDS-R3, R3.1)

Generated by `scripts/cards/paper.mjs`. **D** marks a number derived; **C** a number chosen here. All sizes in mm unless marked; positions from each slip's top-left corner.

Three PDFs, vector, single-sided A4, black ink only, EB Garamond Regular (the cards' own file). Nothing is set under 8 pt: `text()` refuses it. They are delivered with the draft release `shorelines-playtest-1`, never committed.

**How a single-sided slip hides by folding.** One fold of a sheet printed on one side shows both printed faces or neither. So a slip is three bands: a flap, a hidden band of the same height, and a visible strip. The flap folds down over the hidden band, printed face to printed face. What shows is the flap's blank back, and below it the visible strip.
