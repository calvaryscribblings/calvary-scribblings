**DRAFT for Ikenna.** It is ratified when Ikenna renames this file to `RATIFIED.md`, or gives a quoted authorisation that is recorded at the rename. It is never renamed on anyone else's judgement.

Generated by `scripts/cards/motif-sheet.mjs` from `cards/data/motif-draft.json`. Every eligible story and Series instalment was re-read in full for R1.1's two new motifs. Every quote below is checked character for character against the text: `story_bodies` for stories, the instalment EPUBs for Series. All reads were read-only.

**Water:** the sea, a river, a lagoon, rain or a flood as a real presence in events. **Grave:** a death, a funeral or a burial the story turns on. The other six motifs are as R1 defined them. Ring order, clockwise from the top: Letter, Secret, Child, Water, Road, House, Debt, Grave.

## The short answer

**The library honestly fills 50 of 54**: 3 picture cards and 47 story cards. **4 slots are left empty, all of them Water.**

- **Water: 2 of 6.** No eligible story turns on water as its main motif. Two carry it honestly as an alternate reading:
  - *Almost Like Her*: rain on an evening walk is what makes him say "You look like her", the story's first real crack.
  - *Widow's Fire*: its first part is "Still Water", and the husband's drowning is what the story turns on. **One flag:** he drowns in a swimming pool, which the definition doesn't name.
  - **Declined:** *The Call*, a reader's fair Water alternate, because the rain is the night's weather while he waits, a mood rather than a presence in events. The R1 reader read it as atmosphere too.
  - The library's strongest Water is Diary of a Lagos 9-5er, Chapter I ("the road was a river"). But its picture card carries Debt, the motif the series turns on. If Ikenna ruled the Diary picture card as Water, Water would be 3 of 6 and Debt would need one more story (*Notes* is the alternate).
- **Grave is the opposite case.** 21 stories now have it as their main motif, so the deck takes 7 and leaves plenty out.
- **House** reaches 6, but only on alternate readings. No story has House as its main motif, and each House card is labelled.

**The question for Ikenna, not something to fix by stretching:** a 50-card deck, or a ruling on Water. That could mean taking pools, or the Diary card, or replacing the motif. Replacing it would rename THE RESCUE and THE ESCAPE.
