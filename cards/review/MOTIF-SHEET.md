## The short answer

**The library cannot honestly fill 54.** It fills **46**, and the other **8** slots are left empty. The ruled shape is six motifs at 7 and two at 6. Six motifs reach 7; Storm and Fire, set as the two 6s, cannot reach them:

- **Storm: 1 of 6** — short by 5. No eligible story turns on weather. The one honest Storm is a Series instalment (Diary of a Lagos 9-5er, Chapter I: the flooded commute), where it is the reader's strong alternate.
- **Fire: 3 of 6** — short by 3. Two eligible stories turn on real fire. The third is a Series instalment, as a fair alternate. Every other "fire" in the library is a metaphor (*Widow's Fire*, for one) or a passing detail.

House reaches 7, but only on alternate readings: no story has House as its PRIMARY motif. Each House card is labelled.

**This is a question for Ikenna, not something to fix by stretching.** Either the deck is 46 cards, or Storm and Fire give way to two motifs the library does carry. The Endings are named by ring neighbours, so replacing a motif renames two Endings.
