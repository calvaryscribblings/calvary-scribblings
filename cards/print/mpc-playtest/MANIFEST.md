## The product and the canvas

- **MPC "Custom Bridge Size Cards", product code PC1201C26-22**, 2.25 × 3.5 in, each card with its own front and back. Checked on MPC's own pages, 20 Oct 2026: "the minimum image upload size requirements are 747 x 1122 pixels (300DPI)", "36 pixels cut off each side", a safe area of "36 pixels each side". (The product page's description also says "56mm x 88mm"; its specifications say 57 × 89 mm and 2.25" × 3.5". The upload numbers are the inch ones.)
- **Upload canvas: 1494 × 2244 px at 600 dpi** (MPC's 747 × 1122 at 300 dpi, doubled). MPC's bleed 72 px and safe margin 72 px (3.048 mm); MPC's trim 57.15 × 88.90 mm.
- **The design is not rescaled.** Its 57 × 89 mm trim is centred on the canvas, and the bleed runs out to fill it: the ground, glow and grain carry on, as does the picture cards' mount. MPC's cut is 0.075 mm outside the design's trim at each side and 0.050 mm inside it at the top and bottom.
- **Export:** JPEG, quality 95, chroma 4:4:4 (no subsampling), an embedded sRGB profile, 600 dpi in the metadata.

## Checks on the exported files

- **Every code decodes from its exported JPEG**, without inversion, to its own URL: 53 of 53 (3 picture cards, 50 story cards).
- **Code modules sit on whole device pixels** on the new canvas: each code's top-left is snapped to the pixel grid as it is drawn (the largest snap was 0.32 px, 0.014 mm), and every module is 6 px square.
- **JPEG against its lossless render:** worst single-channel difference **31/255** (35-front-the-girl-who-sang-through-the-dark.jpg); the mean absolute difference over all 64 images is at most 0.702/255.
- **Total size: 19.7 MB** for 64 images.
